Biography

Yemi Laja

Founder & Director — Mastercraft Global Ltd

Yemi Laja is a multipotentialite with over 10 years of experience as a singer, songwriter, theatre actor, theatre producer, vocal coach, and creative entrepreneur. She has performed as a singer and actor on high-profile, global stages in Nigeria, the United Kingdom, and Ireland.

She is a valued member of the renowned Crescent Theatre in Birmingham — one of the UK's longest-standing and most respected theatres, known for its rich legacy of outstanding theatrical productions and community engagement.

Beyond performance, Yemi is a qualified lawyer (Barrister and Solicitor of the Federal Republic of Nigeria) and holds a Second Class Upper degree from Durham University — a combination that informs her uniquely structured, integrity-driven approach to coaching and creative enterprise.
